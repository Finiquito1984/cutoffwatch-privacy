Privacy Policy — CutoffWatch

Last updated 25 November 2026.

CutoffWatch – Script Tag & Leftover Code Check for Shopify ("the extension") is an independent browser extension published by Artur Santos. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Shopify.

What the extension does

Storefront scan. When you click the extension icon on a Shopify storefront and press Scan this storefront, the extension reads that page's public HTML, the store's public /llms.txt and /agents.md files, and the browser's resource timing for that page. It then shows, on your computer, which apps load through script tags, which code looks left over from removed apps, and some speed checks.

Theme audit. When you click the icon in your own Shopify admin and press Audit this store's theme, the extension reads your theme files, installed apps and script tags from the Shopify admin tab you have open, using the session you are already signed in with. Findings, diffs and reports are built locally.

Changes to your theme happen only when you confirm them: by default on a new copy of your published theme, never on the published theme unless you tick a confirmation box (a backup copy is made first). Every change is recorded on your device so you can undo it.

Data we collect

None. We do not operate a server for the extension. Your theme files, store data, scan reports and Shopify credentials are never sent to us.

  • The extension never sees or stores your Shopify password, and never logs session tokens.
  • All analysis (scanning, theme audit, diffs, ZIP and reports) happens locally in your browser.
  • Requests go only to the storefront you are viewing (same site) or to the Shopify admin you are signed in to, and only when you start a scan, audit or change.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising.

Data stored on your device

In your browser's local extension storage only: the last scan and audit results (so the report pages can open them); the undo journal with the previous content of files the extension changed; your optional report branding (name, logo, colour); and your plan status from the payment provider once Pro is active. You can delete this data by removing the extension.

Payments

If you buy Pro, payment is handled by ExtensionPay, under its own privacy policy. ExtensionPay (and its payment processor, Stripe) may receive your email address and payment details to process the purchase and verify your licence. We never receive your card details. The extension only receives a yes/no paid status.

Pro costs 7.99 USD per month or 34 USD once (lifetime). Scans, the full audit, diff previews and patch downloads stay free.

Contact form

The form below (and the same form in the extension Settings) sends your subject, message, and optional reply address through a form-forwarding service (Web3Forms, under that service's privacy policy) so the publisher can read your message. This page does not publish a contact email address. If you include a reply address, it is used only to answer you.
